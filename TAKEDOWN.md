# Tebaro Source Review / Takedown Process

If you are a source owner or authorized representative and believe a private collector input is being used contrary to your rights, terms, or technical-access policy, please request review through the repository's Issues channel.

A useful request should identify:

- the source or property you represent;
- your relationship to that source;
- the concern or applicable policy/term;
- any preferred technical or licensing path, if one exists;
- a reliable way for the maintainer to follow up.

A credible request may result in temporary suspension of the source while the concern is reviewed. Where continued use is not appropriate or permission cannot be established, the source should be removed from the collector.

The public coefficient feed does not expose provider identities, raw source values, or provider URLs.
